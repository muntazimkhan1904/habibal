How a Habib Al Ruh Scent Is Built

Every fragrance we release follows the same discipline: a bright opening, a warm heart, and an oud base that outlasts the evening. Nothing is added that doesn't earn its place.

Top

The First Ten Minutes

Saffron, pink pepper, and bergamot open every blend — a bright signal before the deeper notes arrive.

Heart

What Lingers on Skin

Rose from Taif, amber, and a trace of leather sit at the centre — this is the note people remember you by.

Base

What Remains by Nightfall

Our Story

Aged in Dubai, Not Rushed

Habib Al Ruh started as a small blending room, not a factory. We still age every base note for weeks before it's allowed near a bottle — because oud that hasn't been given time smells expensive, but oud that has been aged smells like something.

Every bottle ships in a lined presentation box with a signed authenticity card, and our concierge team is one WhatsApp message away for sampling, gifting, or a private consultation.
